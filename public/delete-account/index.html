CREW CHIEF

Delete your account

CREW CHIEF is provided by Nimbus Engineering.

The fastest option is in the app: sign in, open Settings → Account, choose Delete Account, and type DELETE. The account, owned cloud records, owned uploads, and CREW CHIEF data on that device are permanently removed.

Shared team history may remain without your identity so another team member's records still make sense. If you own a shared team, ownership passes to another member.

Cannot sign in?

Submit a deletion request using the email address on the account. You can also use this form for another privacy question. We will verify ownership before deletion. Do not include passwords or sensitive racing information.

Deletion is permanent. If verification cannot be completed, the request may be refused to protect the account.
